To my wonderful parents,

Dear Mom and Dad,

I know I don’t always show it, and sometimes my anger gets the best of me. I’m sorry for the moments when I make things hard. Thank you for forgiving me, being patient with me, and loving me through every version of myself.

Every morning, you get me ready for the day. You drive me to school and make sure I have something delicious to eat. Those things might seem like part of the routine, but I notice them. I feel your love in every ride, every meal, and every little thing you do for me.

There is so much more I wish I could fit into this letter. I may not always find the right words, but I hope you know how grateful I am to have you. Thank you for being my comfort, my support, and the people who never stop showing up for me.

I love you both more than I can say. I’ll keep trying to be kinder and to show you how much you mean to me.

Forever grateful, always your kid ♥

♥All my love, today and every day.
Made with love, just for you
